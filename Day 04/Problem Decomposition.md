# Logic and Problem-Solving — Day 4: Problem Decomposition

**Today's focus:** break big problems into smaller, manageable parts using two strategies.

## [[Problem Decomposition]]
| Strategy | Direction | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Top-Down** | Big → small | Start with the whole problem and keep splitting it into smaller sub-problems until each is simple enough to solve |
| **Bottom-Up** | Small → big | Start by listing the small pieces and tasks you know are needed, then group them into larger parts until they form the whole solution |

## To Know
- Practice both strategies on the same problem to see how they differ

## Homework
- Decompose these three problems top-down **and** bottom-up: build a house, plan a vacation to a country of your choice, design a social media website
